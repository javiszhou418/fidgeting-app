# Environment quality loop

## Frozen baseline

- Keep the approved Fan Card geometry, material, 80 mm camera language, and
  same-scene contact lighting.
- Preserve
  `Artifacts/FanCardBannerConcept/fan-card-banner-blender-charcoal-preview.png`
  as the calm baseline. Do not overwrite it.

## Failure

The baseline stops at product plus support. It lacks foreground occlusion,
background scale, staged light, particles, and a card-table story. It reads as
lookdev, not a click-driving game Banner.

## Diagnosis

Primary gap: scene assembly and marketing hierarchy. Secondary gap: VFX and
luxury look calibration. Product geometry, camera language, and material
integration are not the failure.

## Skill decision

The current stack is sufficient:

- `frontend-design` + `bolder`: one focal point, asymmetric depth, limited copy.
- `genre-card-party-idle`: readable card-table stage.
- `scene-assembly`: explicit foreground, hero, background, light, and FX groups.
- `camera-cinematography`: foreground occlusion and depth separation.
- `lighting` + `reference-look-calibration`: motivated black-gold casino light.
- `vfx-fx`: bounded suit fragments, chips, and gold dust.
- `rendering`: Cycles beauty plus emission, depth, and Cryptomatte passes.

No generic skill patch is needed. Repair only the environment revision.

## Repair gate

Approve a layered scene contract before any detailed build. Effects must frame
the product, never cover ranks, the central spade, the bearing cap, or the two
air gaps.
