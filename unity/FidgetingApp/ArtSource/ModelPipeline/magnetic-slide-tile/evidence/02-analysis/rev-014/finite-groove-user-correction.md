# Gate 02 rev 14 user-authoritative finite-groove stop correction

Date: 2026-08-27

DecisionState: rev 13 stop logic rejected; rev 12 carrier/groove ownership retained.

The two top-underside grooves have finite physical start and end points. Slide
stops occur when the rotating-carrier followers reach those endpoints. A
numeric range alone is not the mechanism.

The closed groove endpoint is the exact aligned top/bottom overlap. The
maximum-open endpoint must keep the fixed axis covered and the followers
engaged. At every legal slide value and all 30 detent angles, the top stays
above the bottom in layer/Z order. Overhang is legal. The bottom must never
clip, mask, contain, or visually wrap the top exterior.

Physical groove length, endpoint shape, follower cross-section, engagement
depth, and minimum cover margin remain unknown until measured or disassembled.
