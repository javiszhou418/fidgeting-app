## Relevant patterns for Fidgeting App

- Preserve a neutral page field; rarity should be secondary border, badge or
  status information rather than a full-card color wash.
- Let the product or material illustration dominate its own space. Avoid
  stacking a decorative background inside a differently sized media container.
- Inventory uses a predictable grid and separates equipment/ownership context
  from raw item storage.
- Crafting surfaces show the recipe/material relationship directly and keep the
  action/cost area stable.
- Store overview selects a product; product detail dedicates most of the screen
  to a faithful preview before presenting the transaction action.

No image in this directory may be copied into the shipping product.
